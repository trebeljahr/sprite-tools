# AI Character

[Open AI Character](/generate)

AI Character generates a character sprite image from a text prompt with
xAI's image models, then hands it to the rest of the tools.

## Who pays for the image

Every image costs money at xAI. You pick one of two ways to pay:

- **Your own xAI key.** Paste a key from [console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai).
  Your browser sends the prompt and the key straight to xAI, and xAI bills
  your account at its list price. You need no account here. The key stays in
  the page's memory and never reaches the sprite-tools server.
- **AI credit.** Sign in on the [account page](/account) and buy credit.
  The server makes the image with its own xAI key and takes xAI's list price
  plus a stated margin from your credit. The page shows the price per image
  before you generate. An account exists only for this credit.

With credit, you pay for an image when it comes back. If xAI refuses the
request or sends no image, the price goes back to your credit. If xAI does
not answer within 90 seconds, it may still have billed, so the price is
kept.

## Workflow

1. Pick who pays.
2. Pick a sprite template and a model.
3. Describe the character.
4. Choose a chroma-key-friendly background color.
5. Generate the image.
6. Download it, or send it to [Background Removal](/docs/web/background-removal).

## Self-hosting

The own-key path needs no server. AI credit needs the accounts server
(`packages/server`) with `XAI_API_KEY`, and Stripe keys to sell credit; see
`docs/accounts-operations.md` in the repo. The CLI package does not include
AI generation.

For offline or scriptable sprite processing, use the [CLI](/docs/cli) after you already have source art.
